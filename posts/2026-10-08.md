## 1. Fifty-Seven New Neighbors in the Deep

A team of scientists has announced that they found 57 new marine species in a deep underwater valley called the Mar del Plata Canyon, off the coast of Argentina. They also identified five new genera, which are groups of related species.

The work was done at an international workshop in Buenos Aires in July 2026. The specimens came mainly from an expedition in August 2025, when a remotely operated vehicle explored the seafloor. Some of the canyons were more than 3,500 metres deep.

The new species include about 20 tiny crustaceans called copepods, 22 echinoderms such as sea cucumbers, and 14 cnidarians, the group that includes jellyfish. One sea cucumber has already become famous. Scientists nicknamed it "Loki" because its horn-like parts remind them of the Norse god. It was filmed alive on the seafloor.

The discoveries were logged on Ocean Census NOVA, an open online platform for newly found marine species. The physical samples will stay in Argentine science collections. Researchers say the deep ocean is still largely unexplored, and many more surprises are likely to be waiting there.

### Vocabulary
1. **canyon** (noun) – a deep, narrow valley with steep sides. *The river has carved a huge canyon over millions of years.*
2. **specimen** (noun) – an example of an animal or plant collected for study. *The museum keeps each specimen in a labelled box.*
3. **expedition** (noun) – an organised journey for a special purpose, such as research. *Their expedition to the Arctic lasted six weeks.*
4. **identify** (verb) – to recognise and name something. *Experts could identify the bird by its song.*
5. **nickname** (verb) – to give someone or something an informal name. *Fans nicknamed the goalkeeper "the Wall".*
6. **platform** (noun) – a website or system where people share or use information. *The teacher uses an online platform for homework.*
7. **largely** (adverb) – mostly; to a great degree. *The island is largely empty of people.*
8. **unexplored** (adjective) – not yet visited or studied. *Large parts of the rainforest are still unexplored.*

### Questions
1. Where were the 57 new species found, and when was the workshop held?
2. Why did scientists nickname one sea cucumber "Loki"?
3. What does the story suggest about the deep ocean?

## 2. Taiwan's New Fighter Jets Finally Land

Taiwan's air force has received the first two of its new F-16V Block 70 fighter jets from the United States. The aircraft landed at Chihhang Air Base in Taitung County at around noon on Friday, 2 October. They had flown from Hawaii, with a stop in Guam. President Lai welcomed their arrival.

The jets are part of an order for 66 aircraft. However, deliveries were expected to begin more than three years ago. Production changes, supply-chain problems, and software issues all caused delays. These two jets were even expected at the end of August, but they stayed in Hawaii for about a month without a clear explanation.

Now, Taiwanese and US personnel will carry out a joint handover. After that, the jets will go through ground checks, and pilots will begin conversion training. Defence Minister Wellington Koo said more aircraft will arrive before the end of the year. According to the new schedule, deliveries will continue through 2027 and finish in 2028.

The jets will be based in Taitung, where they are expected to strengthen air defence in the eastern part of Taiwan.

### Vocabulary
1. **take delivery of** (phrase) – to officially receive something that was ordered. *The airline took delivery of three new planes.*
2. **order** (noun) – a request to buy a certain number of goods. *The shop placed a large order for winter coats.*
3. **supply chain** (noun) – the network of people and companies that make and move a product. *The storm disrupted the supply chain for fresh fruit.*
4. **handover** (noun) – the act of giving responsibility for something to someone else. *The handover of the keys took five minutes.*
5. **conversion training** (noun phrase) – training that teaches someone to use a new type of equipment. *Pilots need conversion training before flying the new model.*
6. **schedule** (noun) – a plan of when things will happen. *The project is running behind schedule.*
7. **strengthen** (verb) – to make something stronger. *Regular exercise strengthens your heart.*
8. **personnel** (noun) – the people who work for an organisation. *Only authorised personnel may enter the lab.*

### Questions
1. Where did the jets land, and where did they fly from?
2. Name two reasons for the long delay.
3. Why might the report say deliveries are "finally" happening? What does the new schedule suggest about the whole order?

## 3. Two Tiger Sisters Make History in Texas

The Fort Worth Zoo in Texas has announced the birth of two Sumatran tiger cubs, the first of their kind ever born there. The sisters, named Barani and Malu, were born on 1 July 2026. Sumatran tigers are critically endangered, so every birth matters.

The cubs' mother, Ndari, is a first-time mum, and their father is Rakan. Zookeepers confirmed that the cubs and their mother were in stable condition, and the family was introduced to the public on 2 October. These are also the first tiger cubs born at the zoo in almost two decades.

The two sisters already have different personalities. Barani, whose name means "brave" in Indonesian, is more active and curious. Malu is calmer and more cautious. At 13 weeks old, each cub weighs about 18 to 20 pounds, roughly the same as a large pumpkin.

The zoo takes part in a cooperative breeding programme with other North American zoos. The cubs will help to grow the population of Sumatran tigers in managed care. They will make their public debut in the tiger habitat in the coming weeks.

### Vocabulary
1. **critically endangered** (adjective phrase) – at very high risk of disappearing forever. *The critically endangered rhino has fewer than a hundred left.*
2. **cub** (noun) – a young tiger, lion, bear, or fox. *The cub followed its mother through the grass.*
3. **stable** (adjective) – not changing for the worse; steady. *The patient is in a stable condition.*
4. **introduce to the public** (phrase) – to show or present for the first time. *The museum introduced its new exhibit to the public on Monday.*
5. **personality** (noun) – the way a person or animal behaves and reacts. *My cat has a shy personality.*
6. **cautious** (adjective) – careful to avoid danger or mistakes. *Be cautious when you cross a busy road.*
7. **breeding programme** (noun phrase) – a planned effort to produce young animals, often to protect a species. *The breeding programme saved the species from extinction.*
8. **debut** (noun) – a first public appearance. *The singer made her debut at the age of sixteen.*

### Questions
1. Why is this birth historic for the Fort Worth Zoo?
2. How are Barani and Malu different from each other?
3. Why do you think zoos join cooperative breeding programmes for Sumatran tigers?

## 4. Tainan Prepares to Move Its Trains Underground

Tainan is getting ready for a big change in its railway. The city government says train services are scheduled to move to a new underground station on 18 October. Another report is more cautious, saying the opening could depend on final safety inspections, so the date may still change.

The underground line is about 8.23 kilometres long and includes two new commuter stations, Linsen and South Tainan. The mayor has said that once the first phase opens, the level crossings, where roads cross train tracks, will disappear. The land above the old tracks will gradually be turned into a long park-like corridor.

What will happen to the old station? Tainan Railway Station opened in 1900, during the period of Japanese rule, and is recognised as a national heritage site. When the new station begins operating, the old building will close for restoration. Passengers will use a different entrance during the work.

For many residents, the project means fewer traffic jams at crossings and a quieter city centre. For history lovers, it means a chance to protect a much-loved landmark.

### Vocabulary
1. **scheduled** (adjective) – planned to happen at a particular time. *The meeting is scheduled for Monday at ten.*
2. **inspection** (noun) – an official check to make sure something is safe or correct. *The bridge passed its yearly inspection.*
3. **level crossing** (noun phrase) – a place where a road crosses a railway on the same level. *Cars must stop at the level crossing when the barrier comes down.*
4. **corridor** (noun) – a long, narrow strip of land or passage. *A green corridor connects the two parks.*
5. **heritage site** (noun phrase) – a place protected because of its historical value. *The old temple is a national heritage site.*
6. **restoration** (noun) – the work of repairing something to its original condition. *The restoration of the painting took two years.*
7. **landmark** (noun) – a well-known building or place that is easy to recognise. *The clock tower is the town's best-known landmark.*
8. **gradually** (adverb) – slowly, over a period of time. *The weather gradually got warmer.*

### Questions
1. When is the new underground station scheduled to open, and why might that date change?
2. What will happen to the land and the level crossings once the first phase opens?
3. How does the plan try to balance modern development with history?
