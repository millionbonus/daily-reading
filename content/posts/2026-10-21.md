## 1. Typhoon Champi Heads Toward Taiwan

A strong typhoon is moving across the Pacific, and Taiwan is watching it closely. Typhoon Champi was about 2,470 km east-southeast of Eluanbi, Taiwan's southernmost tip, on Sunday afternoon. It was moving west-northwest at 20 km/h. Its maximum winds had reached 191 km/h.

The Central Weather Administration (CWA) says a sea warning could be issued as early as Wednesday night. A land warning might follow on Thursday. Forecasters expect the storm to have its greatest impact on Friday and Saturday, with heavy rain in central and northern Taiwan, as well as Yilan and Hualien counties.

The exact path is still uncertain. Forecast models disagree, and some suggest the typhoon could make landfall in eastern Taiwan. The key factor is the Pacific high-pressure system, a large area of high air pressure that acts like a wall and steers storms. If it weakens early, Champi could turn north before it reaches Taiwan. If it stays strong, the typhoon could take a more southern route, possibly through the Bashi Channel between Taiwan and the Philippines.

Forecaster Liu Pei-teng said Tuesday would be a decisive day for deciding whether the storm turns north. Until then, the weather in Taiwan should stay mostly stable, with only a few brief showers in the northeast and southeast. Residents are advised to follow official updates in the coming days.

## 2. Yelp's AI Will Call the Restaurant for You

Have you ever arrived at a popular restaurant and found a long line? Yelp wants to help you avoid that. The company has launched a feature called "Check for Me" in its app.

Here is how it works. On a restaurant's page, you enter your party size and your phone number. An AI voice agent, which is software that can speak on the phone, then calls the restaurant and asks about the current wait time. After the call, Yelp sends you a text message with the answer. You can then decide whether to go.

The idea is simple, but it raises an interesting question. Some restaurants already use their own AI agents to answer the phone. That means one AI could end up talking to another AI. As TechCrunch noted, a conversation between two agents could easily break down.

Yelp also announced other updates to its AI assistant. It can now work with OpenTable, so users can book a table without leaving the app. It also has memory, which means it can recall preferences from earlier chats. Users can see the past 30 days of chat history, and they can ask for reminders, such as weekend ideas every Friday.

For businesses, Yelp is launching a paid tool called Smart Lead Reply. It answers customer messages automatically and costs $30 per month. Yelp has not said how accurate the wait-time calls are.

1. **party size** (noun phrase): the number of people in a group. *Please tell us your party size when you book.*
2. **voice agent** (noun phrase): a program that talks with people by voice. *A voice agent answered the phone and gave me the opening hours.*
3. **break down** (phrasal verb): to fail or stop working. *The talks broke down after two hours.*
4. **integration** (noun): a connection that lets two systems work together. *The integration lets me pay without leaving the app.*
5. **recall** (verb): to remember. *Can you recall where you parked the car?*
6. **lead** (noun): a person who might become a customer. *The shop gets many leads from its website.*
7. **qualify** (verb): to decide if someone meets certain requirements. *The sales team qualifies each lead before calling.*

## 3. A Towering Fungus, Born After the Dinosaurs

Scientists in China have found a giant fungus that belongs to a completely new family. The fungus, named *Dianjunus rex*, was found growing under trees in Yunnan Province in southwestern China. Researchers from the Kunming Institute of Botany examined five collections from three sites. Their study was published in *Frontiers in Fungal Biology*.

The fungus is impressive in size. Its pale yellow fruiting columns, the parts that rise above the ground, stand about 20 cm tall. The largest ones reach about 50 cm if you include the long black stalk buried underground. A big specimen can weigh about 2.2 kg.

DNA tests showed that it does not fit into any known family. *D. rex* is the only known member of its own group. The team estimates that this family split from its closest relatives about 65 million years ago, around the time the dinosaurs went extinct.

The fungus has few genes for breaking down plant material. The researchers suggest that it may live inside the roots of healthy trees and begin feeding on them only after the tree dies. However, this idea has not been confirmed. They also think its height may help it spread spores, but that also needs testing.

The discovery shows how much remains unknown. Yunnan has more than 6,000 recorded fungus species, and the true number may be over 100,000.
