## A Terminal Under a Cloud-Shaped Sky

Taiwan's biggest airport project is getting close to the finish line. Transportation Minister Chen Shih-kai said on Tuesday that Terminal 3 at Taoyuan International Airport will open on Dec. 25, 2027. A trial run will take place in November of that year. He made the comments while inspecting the terminal's main facilities.

The project is now about 91 percent complete. The main structure was finished in the first half of 2026. Work in the second half of the year has focused on the interior. All 95,000 cloud-shaped ceiling panels are in place. Six check-in counter islands in one part of the departure hall are also done. The baggage claim area will have 12 carousels. Together, they will be as long as all the baggage carousels in Terminals 1 and 2 combined.

Chen said most hardware should be installed by early 2027, and the project remains on schedule. The next phase may be just as hard as building. Getting ready to operate the terminal will mean working with 240 organizations and about 35,000 workers. Preparations will begin in the second half of 2027.

## Dutch Authority to Shoppers: Skip the Smart Glasses

The Dutch Data Protection Authority has a clear message for shoppers: do not buy AI-enabled camera glasses. These are internet-connected glasses that can record video all the time. The authority says they harm the privacy of people nearby and put their users at risk. Its statement falls just short of an outright ban. It also says that using such glasses to record people in public is "almost always prohibited."

Critics have given the devices an unkind nickname, "pervert glasses". They say the glasses can be used to film people without permission, especially to harass women. Some models have a light that shows when they are recording. Privacy advocates say these warnings are not easy enough to notice. Some people have even made phone apps that detect when someone nearby is wearing smart glasses.

The authority also tested the glasses and found they can give wrong and unsafe answers. In one test, a pair gave dating advice to an adult who said they were interested in a minor. The same glasses misread a medicine label. They also claimed to have called an ambulance when they had not.

The advice follows a similar step by Norway. In September, its government said it was considering a ban on wearable devices with cameras.

## Silver Medal, Big Confidence

Taiwan's under-19 boys' table tennis team has won silver at the 2026 ITTF World Youth Championships in Istanbul, Turkey. On Tuesday, China beat Taiwan 3-1 in the final. The match lasted 2 hours and 16 minutes. It is Taiwan's best result at the event since the format changed in 2021.

The silver-medal team was Hung Che-yen, Lin Ching-ting, Yang Hao-jen and Cheng Min-hsiu. Taiwan started well. Hung won the first match against China's Li Hechen in four games. Then China won the next three. Lin lost to Wen Ruibo, who is described as the world youth No. 2. Yang also lost in four games. In the fourth match, Hung pushed Wen to a deciding fifth game, but lost it 2-11.

Head coach Chin Mao-cheng said he was satisfied with the players. He believes the medal will boost their confidence before the individual events. He said China was a very tough opponent and told the players to enjoy the competition.

Other Taiwan teams did well too. The U19 girls reached the quarterfinals, and both the U15 boys and U15 girls reached the semifinals and won bronze.
