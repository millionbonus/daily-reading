## A Very Heavy Passenger

A 15-year-old white rhinoceros named Arirang is about to make a long trip. Leofoo Village Theme Park in Hsinchu County said on Monday that the animal will leave Taiwan on Tuesday for South Korea. There, it will join another white rhino at Everland, the country's largest theme park, as part of a breeding program.

Arirang weighs about 2,000 kilograms. The park says it is the largest and heaviest animal of a near-threatened species ever exported from Taiwan. Moving it will not be easy. There are no direct cargo flights between Taiwan and South Korea, so the rhino will travel through Hong Kong. The flight alone takes about 8.5 hours. Including ground transport, Arirang will spend around 17 hours inside its crate.

Wu Hsuan-yi, who leads the park's white rhino conservation project, called Arirang "something of a troublemaker". He added that the rhino gets along well with its caregiver.

Leofoo looks after 22 white rhinos, the second-largest group in Asia. In 2021, it sent a female rhino named Emma to Japan. The park's foundation plans to expand its rhino program in 2027, and Vietnam is expected to be the next international partner.

The effort matters because wild numbers are falling. The IUCN lists southern white rhinos as near threatened. The wild population was estimated at 15,752 at the end of 2024, which was 11.2% lower than in 2023.

## AI Is Creating Jobs Far Beyond Tech

Artificial intelligence (AI) is not only changing the tech industry. A new report says it is also pushing up hiring in many other fields. The report was released on Monday by Taiwan's Institute for Information Industry (III) and the online job bank 1111.

The biggest rise came from an unexpected place. Job openings at chemical-related manufacturers grew by 74.32% in the third quarter of 2026, compared with the first quarter. That was the highest growth among major industries in Taiwan. These companies make glass cloth and copper-clad laminate (CCL), materials used in electronic circuit boards. Warehousing and transport support services rose by 44.83%, and architectural planning and design rose by 24.57%.

Jobs close to AI also grew quickly. Openings for AI engineers went up by 66.12%, and algorithm development engineers by 54.41%. Cybersecurity engineers rose by 35.47%. Semiconductors grew by a smaller 13.63%.

Lin Yu-fan, deputy executive director of the III, said workers in the AI era need four qualities: "Imagination," "Integration," "Impact," and "Investment."

The message is clear. The AI boom is creating demand across the whole supply chain, from factories to trucks to design offices.
