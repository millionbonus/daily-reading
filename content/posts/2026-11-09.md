## 1. A Taste of Hong Kong in Taipei

A bazaar in Taipei this weekend showed that ordinary people can stay close even when governments keep their distance. The fourth annual Hong Kong-themed bazaar took place on Saturday and Sunday at Huashan 1914 Creative Park. It was organized by a government-funded group and had about 70 stalls selling Hong Kong-style food and creative products.

Official ties between Taiwan and Hong Kong have weakened since the 2019 protests and the 2020 national security law. Hong Kong suspended its representative office in Taipei in 2021. Taiwan's office in Hong Kong still operates, but it is run by locally hired staff.

Even so, visitors and vendors said personal links are strong. Jojo Man, a florist from Hong Kong who moved to Taiwan four years ago, returned for her third year. She said making money "comes second" for her. Ian Yip, a software engineer who has lived in Taiwan for more than 20 years, said Taiwanese people are now more accepting than before. Early on, he was told that some people "didn't like Hongkongers". Those stereotypes have faded, he said.

An official said more than 13,000 people from Hong Kong and Macao have become Taiwanese citizens since 2019. The event drew nearly 67,000 visits on Saturday. That was lower than the nearly 90,000 visits over both days last year.

## 2. The Plain Kindle Is Enough

Amazon's cheapest Kindle is not fancy, but one reviewer thinks it is good enough for most readers. Amazon updated its Kindle range in October. The new models have an edge-to-edge screen design, and the Paperwhite and Colorsoft models are lighter and thinner.

Prices have gone up by $40 to $50 across the range. The article says the main cause is a global shortage of memory chips, which has been driven by the boom in artificial intelligence (AI). The basic six-inch Kindle now starts at $169.99 with ads.

The new basic model is 11% lighter and 14% thinner than the 2024 version. It weighs 140 grams, down from 158 grams. Amazon says books open 30% faster, although that is the company's own claim. Battery life is about six weeks. That is shorter than the Paperwhite (12 weeks) and the Colorsoft (8 weeks).

The basic Kindle lacks several extras. It has no warm light, no colour screen, no automatic brightness and no waterproofing. Still, the reviewer's advice is clear: buy it if you can live without those features. An aluminium version costs $189.99, and a 16 GB Paperwhite costs $199.99.

## 3. Festival of Lights, and a Flight Route Returns

Crowds gathered at Tamsui Fisherman's Wharf in New Taipei on Saturday for this year's Diwali festival. Diwali is known as the Festival of Lights. It stands for the victory of light over darkness and good over evil. The event ran from 4 p.m. to 8:30 p.m. and offered Indian music, dance and food, including naan and curry.

The festival is held every year by the India Taipei Association, which serves as India's representative office in Taiwan, together with the New Taipei City government. At the opening ceremony, Taiwanese officials lit traditional oil lamps with India's representative, Ninad Deshpande.

Deshpande said the event was a milestone, because EVA Air will launch direct Taipei–New Delhi flights on December 1. The route has been missing for six years. China Airlines flew it until 2020, when it was suspended during COVID-19. Deshpande called the new service "a vital bridge" for business, tourism and culture. The association gave away two round-trip tickets at the festival.

One highlight was a student band from Taipei Municipal Chenggong High School. The band has a history of more than 70 years, and it played classic Indian pieces for the crowd.
