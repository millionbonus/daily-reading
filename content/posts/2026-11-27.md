## 1. Sunshine Expected for Election Day

Taiwan has had a damp week, but the weather is about to improve. The Central Weather Administration (CWA) says scattered showers fell on Thursday. As the northeasterly winds weaken, temperatures are rising.

On Friday, the air will stay fairly humid. Showers are likely along the Keelung coast and in the mountains of the north and northeast. Most other areas will be partly cloudy to sunny.

The big day is Saturday, when Taiwan holds its elections. The forecast is good news for voters. The rain will ease, and most of the island will be sunny to partly cloudy. A few showers may still appear early in the morning on the Keelung coast and in the mountains around Greater Taipei. Parts of the east and south may also see brief showers.

Looking further ahead, a northeast monsoon will reach Taiwan on Sunday. It should weaken on Monday. Mornings will stay cool, but most areas will be dry. Independent meteorologist Wu Der-rong said temperatures should climb on Sunday, with daytime highs of about 30 degrees Celsius.

So, if you plan to vote on Saturday, you probably won't need an umbrella. It may still be wise to bring a light jacket, just in case the morning feels cool.

2. **humid** (adjective): having a lot of water in the air, so it feels sticky. *The summer in Taipei is hot and humid.*
3. **ease** (verb): to become less strong or less severe. *The pain began to ease after he took a rest.*
4. **monsoon** (noun): a seasonal wind that often brings heavy rain or cool air. *The winter monsoon makes the north coast windy.*
5. **forecast** (noun/verb): a prediction about what will happen, especially the weather. *The forecast says it will be sunny tomorrow.*
6. **give way to** (phrasal verb): to be replaced by something else. *The cloudy morning gave way to a bright afternoon.*
7. **localized** (adjective): limited to a small area. *The storm caused localized flooding near the river.*

## 2. Smaller Chips, Smarter Phones: NTU's Energy-Saving AI Processors

Many people use AI tools through the internet. Your question travels to a faraway data center, and the answer comes back. That process uses a lot of energy, and it can be slow. Researchers at National Taiwan University (NTU) want to change this.

A team led by electrical engineering professor Yang Chia-hsiang has built two generations of energy-efficient chips. These chips let generative AI (software that creates text or images) run directly on a device. This is called "on-device" AI. The National Science and Technology Council announced the work on Wednesday.

The chips are "Transformer learning processors." A Transformer is the type of AI model behind many chatbots. The team's chips avoid unnecessary calculations. The council compared this to reading a long article by first finding the keywords and main points.

The first chip reached 99.2 tera operations per second per watt, which measures how much work a chip does for each unit of power. The second chip reached 157. According to the council, that is up to 7.6 times better than other advanced chips reported at the time.

On-device AI could help phones, robots, and factory machines respond quickly. It also keeps private data on the device. The team's goal, Yang said, is to "compute only what is necessary and move less data." A third chip, made on a 16-nanometer process in October 2026, is now being developed.

### Vocabulary
1. **energy-efficient** (adjective): using little energy to do a job. *An energy-efficient fridge can lower your electricity bill.*
2. **on-device** (adjective): happening on your own phone or computer, not on a distant server. *The app offers on-device translation, so it works without Wi-Fi.*
3. **generative AI** (noun phrase): AI that can create new content, such as text or pictures. *Generative AI can write a short poem in a few seconds.*
4. **processor** (noun): the part of a computer that carries out instructions. *A faster processor makes the laptop feel quicker.*
5. **unnecessary** (adjective): not needed. *Turn off unnecessary lights to save power.*
6. **latency/delay** (noun): the waiting time before something responds. *A long delay made the video call hard to follow.*
7. **autonomously** (adverb): without help from a person. *The robot moved autonomously through the warehouse.*
8. **bandwidth** (noun): the amount of data that can be moved in a given time. *Low bandwidth made the video freeze.*

## 3. The Cybertruck Is Gone, but Buyers Keep Grabbing It

Tesla's Cybertruck has an odd story. The angular electric pickup has divided opinion since its launch. Now Tesla plans to end its production in 2027. Yet used Cybertrucks are selling faster than any other used truck in America.

According to data from the car-research site iSeeCars.com, a used Cybertruck stayed on the market for an average of 39.1 days in October 2026. All used trucks together averaged 65.5 days. The Cybertruck even beat the Ford Maverick Hybrid and the Rivian R1T.

Why the rush? One reason may be that supply will soon become limited. When Tesla stops making new ones, the used ones may become harder to find. An analyst quoted in the report expects used sales to rise as the supply shrinks.

Fuel prices may also play a part. The report says high gasoline prices are pushing more buyers toward electric vehicles and hybrids.

So a truck that many people mocked is, for now, a hot item on the used market. Some buyers may see it as a future collector's piece. Others may simply want a vehicle that is different from everything else on the road.

### Vocabulary
1. **angular** (adjective): having sharp corners and straight lines. *The building has an angular, modern design.*
2. **divide opinion** (phrase): to make people disagree. *The new logo has divided opinion among fans.*
3. **on the market** (phrase): available to buy. *The house has been on the market for two months.*
4. **supply** (noun): the amount of something available. *Bad weather reduced the supply of fresh vegetables.*
5. **shrink** (verb): to become smaller. *The company's profits began to shrink last year.*
6. **mock** (verb): to laugh at someone or something in an unkind way. *Classmates used to mock his old bicycle.*
7. **collector's piece** (noun phrase): an item people want to keep because it is rare. *The first-edition comic became a collector's piece.*
